## Fonts

- `fonts/web/fonts.css`
- `fonts/variable/Rubik[wght].ttf`
- `fonts/OFL-Rubik.txt`

All logo SVGs are flat filled paths (no live text, no strokes, no masks) in exact brand colors, transparent except the app tiles and avatars. `light` files are for light backgrounds and `dark` for dark ones; `mono` and `mono-dark` are one-color drawings, not recolors. The `symbol-small` files are the symbol's small master, for use below 32 px tall. `icon/favicon.svg` swaps its fills with the device theme; the ICO is the light drawing only. Fonts are SIL Open Font License 1.1 (license in `fonts/`): `fonts/web` holds the book's subsets (Latin Rubik, and Rubik Arabic for the examples, where the Arabic subset is exactly the characters of the book's examples) and `fonts/variable` the full Rubik file, which also carries the Arabic letters. This edition has no logo animation and no sound files. The records file the application pages are drawn from is handed over beside this folder, not inside it.
